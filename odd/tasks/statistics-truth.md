# Statistics that tell the truth: /statistics total, risk and trade figures
Branch `fix/statistics-numbers` (from `master` dc94449). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Runner: vitest (`npm test`, `npm run type-check`).

## Specs

- S1 (total): "Te podes fijar porque en la pagina /statistics el valor total de la cartera es menor al que aparece en dashboard" / "pero si en dashboard pongo dolar cable el valor no es el mismo" → the /statistics headline "Valor total de la cartera" shows exactly the dashboard's total in the currency the viewer chose on the dashboard (Pesos, Dólar MEP, Dólar cable), and the composition (Acciones, ONs, Efectivo) adds up to it in that same currency.
- S2 (prices): /statistics never values a stock at cost only because its quote was not in the cache: missing quotes are fetched as the dashboard fetches them.
- S3 (audit): "y de paso fijate los indicadores de riesgo y demas, no pueden decir cualquier cosa." → every figure on /statistics (risk metrics, evolution, benchmark, trade and position statistics, per-class results) means what its label says; findings are listed in the Log and each WRONG one is fixed or reported.

- S4 (risk base): the whole-portfolio risk metrics (Sharpe, volatility, max drawdown), its evolution chart and its benchmark comparison never rest on days whose recorded cash is negative (deposits missing): they start the day after the last negative-cash day and say from which date; with negative cash today they show no number and say why ("faltan depósitos").
- S5 (risk honesty): the screen says what each risk figure is and how much it rests on: Sharpe without a risk-free rate is labelled so; volatility and Sharpe need a minimum sample before a number is shown; the period measured (from date, number of days) is stated; a degraded series (prices missing, valued at cost) and skipped intervals are disclosed.
- S6 (benchmark): the comparison is like for like: dividends on both sides (total-return SPY and QQQ), or the index is labelled as price-only (Merval).
- S7 (trade stats honesty): on the Acciones and ONs tabs, "Resultado" includes dividends and coupons like the headline; "Resultado por activo" is one bar per asset (open plus realized) and keeps the losses; a figure with no data shows "—", never 0; holding days say what they measure; unpriced positions are disclosed; the ONs tab never claims "USD, mercados internacionales"; monthly results show every month with its year; best and worst say they mix open positions and sales; closed-trade statistics are not under "Riesgo"; a stock missing from the price cache is fetched (S2).
- S8 (matured ON): an ON whose capital was fully amortized leaves the open positions: no value, no result, no position count.
- S9 (capital line): the user chose "Plata que pusiste (Recommended)" — "Depósitos − retiros en \"Toda la cartera\": lo ganado se ve como diferencia sobre lo aportado. En Acciones/ONs pasa a decir \"Costo de lo que tenés\". El \"Capital invertido\" del dashboard (costo + caja) también lo renombro para que no diga dos cosas distintas." → on /statistics "Toda la cartera" the evolution chart's second line is the money put in: the value on the first measured day (S4) plus each deposit and minus each withdrawal after it, labelled "Plata que pusiste"; on Acciones/ONs that line is the cost of the open positions, labelled "Costo de lo que tenés"; the dashboard's "Capital invertido" figure and its chart line are renamed "Costo + caja" (computation unchanged) and its tooltip says "resultado abierto".
- S10 (deposit dates): the user chose "la b, pero hagamoslo con cuidado y lo mejor posible, sin errores" — option 2 as offered: "La app asume que cada vez que la caja iba a quedar negativa entró justo la plata que faltaba ese día, y la descuenta de esos 4 depósitos. El total que pusiste no cambia, solo se adelantan las fechas." → without touching stored data, the derived history moves, from the nearest later external deposit(s), exactly the shortfall of every day whose recorded cash would end negative to that day; the total deposited, the trade legs, the withdrawals and the income never change; the daily cash and the deposit/withdrawal flows the whole portfolio's risk, benchmark and money-put-in line neutralize come from the same adjusted movements; a shortfall no later deposit covers stays negative (S4 still applies); the screen says how much was moved and from when.
- S11 (missing prices, quietly): "Dale, pero que no lo grite, sino que esté con con i y q sea hover y ahí salga lo q pasa" → the risk row's warning "Faltan precios de algunos días: se valuaron al costo." (one sentence for eight different causes, wrong for most) becomes a quiet line with an (i): hovering it (desktop) or tapping it (touch) shows exactly what was missing, per asset, in words that match each cause (no live quote today, no price history, stale Yahoo bars, unknown splits, missing CCL, unreadable ON closes, unreadable ON schedules, a history cut short).

Assumptions: minimum samples before a risk number is shown: volatility ≥ 60 return intervals, Sharpe ≥ 126 (parent's default, S5). Closed-trade statistics stay per sale and are relabelled "ventas" (round trips not implemented). The dashboard's currency choice (`resumen:currency`, default MEP) is the one /statistics uses; per-class "Resultado" includes dividends and coupons, as the dashboard's "Realizado, con dividendos y cupones" does.
