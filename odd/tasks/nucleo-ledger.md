# Single ledger core: today and history from the same computation

Objective: every user, with any asset and any date, sees their real history at market, derived from the ledger, with "today" and "history" being one computation.
Branch: `feat/ledger-core` (from `feat/dashboard-layout`). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Test runner: vitest (`npm test`, `npm run type-check`).

## Specs

- S1 (mission): "lo quiero hacer bien, acordate la mision de hacer un portfolio profesional, para eso no tenemos que pensar solo en mi caso, sino en una solucion integral para usuarios futuros. Asi con todo" → designed for any user, any ON, any date; no single-user patches.
- S2 (expected result): "la historia real tal cual fue, como si lo hubiese completado dia a dia. Que muestre los datos verdaderos, sin mentir, todo bien completo, tal cual fue" → history is derived from the ledger plus market data of each day, and a late load, edit or delete of an old operation rewrites the past accordingly.
- S3 (missing price): "la 1 sin marca sino" (option 1: last traded price) → on a day with no trade, value at the last traded close, with no visual marker. Assumption: before an asset's first trade after purchase, use the purchase price.
- S4 (coupons and amortizations): "la 1. Acordate portfolio profesional completo, hacemos las cosas bien." (option 1: automatic from the bond schedule, user can correct one) → past and future ON coupons and amortizations are generated from the payment schedule and the nominal held at the record date; the user can override one payment's amount or mark it not received.
- S5 (scope): "Si quiero ahcerlo completo y bien hecho desde ya, cueste lo que cueste" and "Si hay que elimianr cosas lo hacemos, dejemos todo prolijo y bien hecho. PROFESIONAL" → one ledger core replaces every duplicated implementation; stored derived state and dead code are removed, not patched around.
- S6 (all-in prices): "capaz si compre mas caro es por comisiones, impuiestos etc, y capaz por tipo de cambio o distinto dolar" → the market price used for valuation (source data) is separate from the price the user paid (ledger, may include fees); cost basis always comes from the ledger.
- S7 (dollar): "No nos cuesta nada preguntar el valor cada vez que se pregunta por las acciones, ademas en argentina se re ve el valor del dolar, ahsta podriamos ponerlo com dato en el dashboard" → one dollar series: today live from dolarapi.com (1-minute cache), closed days from `fx_rates`; the GGAL-implied CCL is removed; the Resumen shows today's MEP and CCL.
- S8 (missing payments): "la 1" (option 1, after asking "pero como sabe la app que le falta pagos") → when no source covers a payment the bond's own calendar says had to exist (or its residual shows an unexplained amortization), the app asks the user for it, pre-filled with an estimate when one exists; it never invents it.

## Design (approved 2026-10-22, "si")

**Principle.** The ledger (`transactions` + `cash_movements` + ON flow overrides) is the only source of truth. Holdings, cost, realized P&L, cash, value, history and statistics are pure functions of the ledger and market data at a date. "Today" is the last date.

**Market data** (global, stored with `source`):
- ON prices: `on_price_history`, backfilled on demand from Rava (`https://www.rava.com/perfil/descarga-historicos/<TICKER>`, raw closes per 100 original VN; `volumen=0` rows are no-trade days and are skipped). Nightly capture continues. BYMA `historical-series` is back-adjusted and must not be used for history.
- Stock prices: Yahoo, as today (splits un-adjusted).
- FX: one series (S7): today live from dolarapi.com, closed days from `fx_rates` (argentinadatos, same provider). The GGAL-implied CCL is removed.
- ON schedules: one helper merging `on_flow_history` (BYMA rows, plus past payments reported by bonistas.com stored with `source='bonistas'`), `on_cashflows` and the backward derivation; `scheduleGaps` names the expected dates no source covers (S8).

**Ledger core** (one pure module): one operation order, one running average cost, one sale validation (cannot sell more than held), shared by the write path. Replaces the five copies (SQL RPC 044, `calculateRunningAvgCost`, `rebuildClosedTrades`, `rebuildON*`, `multicurrency.replayLedger`).

**Automatic flows.** Coupons and amortizations are generated by the core at computation time (schedule × VN held at record date), not stored. Overrides (amount or not received) live in a per-user table. Existing manual CUPON rows migrate to overrides and are deleted. Amortization is a return of capital: cash in, cost reduced by the amortized fraction, quantity unchanged (prices are per 100 original VN).

**Valuation.** One rule: last traded close on or before the date (S3); never traded since purchase → purchase price.

**Reads.** Resumen, Rendimiento, Estadísticas, ONs and the trading sidebar read the core through the existing write-invalidated Redis cache.

**Removed.** Tables `positions`, `on_positions`, `closed_trades`, `on_closed_trades`, `cash_balance`, `portfolio_snapshots`; the snapshot job, backfill and its script; `series-provenance`, retroactive-entry warnings; the four missing-price policies; the GGAL CCL. The RPC is reduced to validate and insert ledger rows. Dropping stored tables is high risk (item 1): backup, core-vs-stored comparison and explicit user OK first.

**Out of scope.** Automatic CEDEAR dividends (US withholding and ratios; own stage later).
