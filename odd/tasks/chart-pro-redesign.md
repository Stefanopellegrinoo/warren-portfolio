# Chart Pro redesign (TradingView-like, more legible)

Objective: the Gráficos screen as approved in the prototype (`.superpowers/brainstorm/3631719-1793294529/content/pro-v5.html`, not committed): every pane named and legible with any number of indicators, TradingView candles/grid/thin lines on Warren's warm background, icon-only app menu on Gráficos, resizable watchlist, indicators kept in the account, phone done right.
Branch: feat/chart-pro-redesign (on master 7f0c7bc). Delivery: ask-on-risk; work-unit commits per task; push/PR/merge are the user's call. Runner: vitest (`npx vitest run`), `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`.

## Specs

- S1 Legible panes. User (L1): "la idea es que sea más legible, más fácil de entender y de usar". Approved in the prototype: each indicator pane carries its own legend (name, parameters, values that follow the crosshair) with actions minimize, hide, settings, remove; indicator lines show no colored value boxes on the price scale (values live in the legend); reference bands RSI 30–70 and Estocástico 20–80, ADX level 25; the price pane keeps about half the height and the indicator panes share the rest equally; a minimized pane becomes a thin labeled strip that keeps the indicator on.
- S2 The user picks the indicators. User (L3): "La cantidad de indicadores que hay las elijo yo, en tradingview como tengo el free tier me dan maximo dos, asi que quioero poder tener siempre la cantidad que o quiero, porque si no voya tener que ponerlo siempre devuelta." No limit and no reduction by the redesign; factory defaults for a new user stay as today. Approved (L6, answer "dale" to "Incluiría además guardar tus indicadores en tu cuenta, para que sean los mismos en el celu y en la compu"): which indicators are on, hidden or minimized lives in the account.
- S3 Nothing removed. User (L3): "Despues me sacaste la lista y el menu. No hiciste una cosa bien, porfavor hacelo devuelta y esta vez bien. Enfocate solo en el pro". The app menu, the watchlist with "Tu posición", the phone bar (Intervalo, Dibujar, Alerta, Indicadores, Lista), the 16 drawing tools, Comparar, alerts, trade markers, the favorites bar and the indicator settings all stay.
- S4 Icon-only app menu on Gráficos. User (L4): "Tambien quiero que el menu (navbar de la app) cuando estamos en el grafico se meta para adentro, osea que solo aparezcan los dibujos". Approved: from 820 px up, on Gráficos only, the menu shows only its icons with the name as a tooltip; a » button pins it open and is remembered; the phone keeps its ☰ drawer; other pages unchanged. Assumption (told to the user, not contradicted): "dibujos" means the icons.
- S5 Resizable list. User (L4): "y tambien que la lista de acciones se pueda agrandar y achicar con el mouse". Approved: drag its left edge, 220–640 px, never leaving the chart under 420 px; the width is remembered on the device; double click goes back to 280 px; wider lists show more (the asset name from 360 px, a one-month line from 460 px).
- S6 Look. User (L5): "algo tiene distinto, nose si es el color de fondo, el color de las velas, nose, pero lo veo mejor. EL resto me gusta como esta" and (L6): "perfecto deja todo menos el color, me gusto mas el color que estaba antes". Approved: Warren's warm background kept (dark and light); TradingView candles up #089981, down #F23645; volume in those colors at ~45 % at the foot of the price pane; soft grid both vertical and horizontal; indicator lines 1 px by default (a style the user saved keeps its width); last-price label in the last candle's color; the drawing rail in five groups (Líneas, Canales y Fibonacci, Formas y texto, Medición, Posiciones) whose flyouts name every tool; range chips 1M…Todo and %/log/auto under the chart; the TradingView credit as a visible link instead of the logo inside the chart.
- S7 Phone. User (L7): "dale y tambien asegurate de hacerlo bien para mobiler". Approved phone layout: header ☰, symbol, price, change, ★; legend in two short rows, candle data only while touching; panes legible with a minimum height; range chips row; phone bar unchanged; sheets for Indicadores, Intervalo, Dibujar. Checked at 11 widths 320–1920 without horizontal scroll and on a short phone viewport (Safari with toolbars).
- S8 No regressions: drawings, alerts, trade markers, compare, favorites bar, indicator settings and select-before-move behave as before; suite, tsc and lint pass.
