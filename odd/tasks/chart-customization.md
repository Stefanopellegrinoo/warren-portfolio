# Chart customization like TradingView

Objective: on Gráficos, select-before-move on touch, drawing settings with remembered styles and a floating favorites bar, indicator style settings, and alerts with TradingView-like conditions; design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-chart-customization-design.md` (read it whole, it is the detailed spec), mockups https://claude.ai/artifact/THwgp6hKAivqthcZgYw8zf.
Branch: feat/chart-customization (on fix/ticker-search 8371bc8, on master 5460328). Delivery: single-pr at the end, user: "Todo junto al final"; one work-unit commit per task. Runner: vitest (`npx vitest run`), `npx tsc --noEmit`, Chromium probe in a `mktemp -d` clone with its own `npm ci`.

## Specs

- S1 Select before move, touch only. User: "Otra cosa es que , para poder mover un dibujo o alerta, primero se toca que presionar lo que se quiera mover, porque ahora cuando voy pasando el dedo y toco uno de estos, se mueve para todos lados". Approved ("Bien segui"): a finger dragging over an unselected drawing or alert pans the chart; a tap selects it (points and its bar); a selected one drags (body moves it, a point reshapes it; an alert changes price); a tap on empty chart or "Listo" deselects. Mouse unchanged (press and drag moves directly). A locked drawing can be selected, not moved. A selected alert shows a bar with ⚙, Borrar and Listo (⚙ opens the S6 sheet; until S6 exists it is absent). Design §1.
- S2 Drawing settings. User: "que se pueda modificar a los indicadores, alertas y dibujos , como en trading view, que se pueda cambiar sus configuraciones, colores, fondos, grosor, obvio que depende de cada indicador y tipo" and "Tipo en el canal paralelo, quería sacarle el fondo, cambiar el color." Approved ("Todo bien, segui"): Fondo button in the selected bar on fillable kinds (fill on/off, color, transparency); ⚙ Configuración opens every option of that kind (sheet on phone, dialog on computer); new drawings use the style last chosen for that tool (user: "Salga igual"); "Volver al de fábrica" resets it. Design §2.
- S3 Favorites bar. User: "También que como en trading view, se puedan fave se dibujos ya aparezcan la barra que se puede mover para todos lados con estos". Approved: a star per tool in the Dibujar list; starred tools in a floating vertical bar with a drag grip; a tap arms the tool; a switch hides the bar; the bar's position is per device. Design §2.
- S4 Account storage. User, asked "¿Querés que tus favoritos y tus estilos sean los mismos en el celu y en la compu?": "Si". Favorites, remembered drawing styles and saved indicator settings live in the account; an indicator's settings reach the account only when the user saves them (devices keep their current values until then); which indicators are on/hidden stays per device. New migration; applied by the user right before the final merge. Design §2, §3.
- S5 Indicator settings. Approved ("Bien segui"): the same sheet with Values (today's numbers) and Style per line (color, width, show/hide); Bollinger fill; RSI levels 70/30 editable with colors and fill; MACD lines and histogram up/down colors; Volume up/down colors; OBV, Stochastic, ADX lines; reset to factory. The three EMAs stay. Design §3.
- S6 Alert sheet and menu. User: "A alertas quiero cuando\nToco para configurar poder poner el color, cambiar el precio manualmente y el tipo de alerta" and "No puede tener tipo cuál es la condicion, tipo precio, alguna media móvil o para usar en algún indicador, después si es cruce ascendente, ascendente o cualquiera de los dos, y el valor" and "Como en trading view". Approved ("Perfecto"): sheet with Cuando (price or an indicator line), Cruce (para arriba / para abajo / cualquiera), Contra (a typed value or another line), Color, a restating sentence, Borrar. Alerta opens a menu: "Tocar el precio en el gráfico", "Nueva alerta…", this ticker's alerts. Indicator alerts keep the chart timeframe and periods of their creation. Existing price alerts keep working unchanged. Design §4.
- S7 Server evaluation. The worker evaluates the new conditions (indicator lines on candles of the alert's timeframe, crossing up/down/either, value or another line), fires once by push and Telegram as today; a condition it cannot compute moves the alert to "No se puede revisar" and notifies once on that change. Design §4.
- S8 No regressions. Desktop pixel-identical where nothing was asked to change; existing drawings, alerts and indicator settings look and behave as before for a user who never opens the new settings; no horizontal scroll 320–1920; PR #31 phone layout kept.
